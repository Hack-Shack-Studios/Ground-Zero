# Ground Zero

## About the Game

Ground Zero is a 3D wave-based first-person survival shooter where an astronaut crash-lands on a hostile alien planet. Surrounded by waves of robotic enemies, the player must defend a central structure known as the Forge.

The Forge is both your lifeline and your greatest asset. Enemies will relentlessly attack it, and if its health reaches zero, the game ends. Defeating enemies earns currency that can be spent at the Forge to purchase perks and upgrades, creating a strategic balance between protecting the structure and strengthening yourself.

Survive increasingly difficult waves, upgrade your abilities, and hold the line against the robotic invasion.
